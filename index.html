What the data changed

＋

I spent the first several months on a deliberate testing phase. I sourced a wide mix of categories, vacuum parts, DVD players, board games, apparel, electronics, to see what actually sold and at what margin. 20 of 409 sales still closed at a loss, about $107 total, and almost all of it came from that early window. Once a category showed weak sell through or thin margin, I cut it from future sourcing. That's a big reason time to sell has come down and profit per sale has gone up ever since.
